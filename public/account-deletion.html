Account & Data

Delete your Gadget Restore account

You can permanently delete your account and the personal data held with it, at any time, from inside the app or by writing to us.

Delete from within the app

  1. Open the Gadget Restore app on your device.
  2. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm when prompted.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. You will be signed out immediately on every device.

If you cannot sign in

If you have lost access to your registered mobile number and cannot sign in, email us from the address registered on your account, or include your registered mobile number so we can identify you. We will verify your identity before deleting anything, and complete the request within 30 days.

What happens to your data

Deleted

  • Your account and sign-in access
  • Name, email address and contact number
  • PAN, GST and business details
  • Your masked Aadhaar reference and its verification record
  • Profile photograph
  • All active sessions, on every device
  • Your referral code and referral links

Retained, where law requires

  • Records of completed payments, for tax and accounting
  • Token wallet ledger entries tied to those payments
  • Device verification records, as an audit trail

Anything retained is kept only for as long as the law requires, is separated from your profile wherever possible, and is deleted once that period expires. Unused token balances are forfeited on deletion and are not refundable.

Before you delete

This cannot be reversed

Once deleted, your verification history and any remaining token balance cannot be recovered, and signing up again with the same mobile number creates a new, empty account. If you have unused tokens or an unresolved dispute, contact support first.
