03 Data we collect

You give us

  • Account: mobile number and country code.
  • KYC — individuals: full name, contact number, email address, PAN, and Aadhaar number for OTP verification.
  • KYC — businesses: company name, contact number, email address, PAN, GST number, and a photograph of the owner.
  • Device checks: the IMEI number(s) of a device, its model, and optionally the name of the customer the device relates to.
  • Profile photo, where you choose to upload one.

Generated as you use the app

  • Verification results returned by the national CEIR database.
  • Token wallet balance and a transaction ledger of every credit and debit.
  • Payment identifiers issued by our payment gateway.
  • Your referral code and, if you were referred, who referred you.
  • A device identifier for each signed-in session, so sessions can be revoked.

Collected automatically

  • IP address, request timestamps and endpoints accessed, kept in server logs for security, abuse prevention and debugging.
What we never store

We do not store your one-time passwords in readable form, your full Aadhaar number, or your card, UPI or bank credentials. Card and UPI details are entered directly with our payment gateway and never reach our servers.

04 Aadhaar and identity verification

Where Aadhaar verification is required, we use the OTP-based e-KYC service operated by the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI), accessed through a UIDAI-authorised verification partner. You enter your Aadhaar number, UIDAI sends a one-time password to your registered mobile number, and you enter that OTP to confirm the number belongs to you.

How your Aadhaar number is handled

Your Aadhaar number is transmitted to the verification partner only for the duration of that check. We do not retain the full number. Once verification completes we store only a masked form showing the last four digits (for example XXXXXXXX1234), plus a one-way cryptographic hash used solely to prevent the same Aadhaar being registered to more than one account. The hash cannot be reversed to recover the number.

Aadhaar verification is used only to establish identity for the services you request. We do not use it for any other purpose, and we do not share Aadhaar data with any third party other than the authorised verification partner performing the check.

Where the app is used to verify the identity of a customer selling a device to you, that customer's Aadhaar is verified in the same way, again without the full number being retained. You are responsible for obtaining that person's consent before initiating such a check.

05 Device and IMEI verification

The core function of the app is to check whether a mobile device has been reported lost, stolen or blocked. To do this we send the IMEI number you enter to the Central Equipment Identity Register (CEIR) operated by the Department of Telecommunications, Government of India, and return the status it reports.

We keep a record of each check — the IMEI numbers, device model, the result returned, the time of the check, and any customer name you chose to enter. This record exists so that you can review your own verification history, and so that we can resolve billing disputes and meet our obligations if a check is later questioned.

06 Payments and the token wallet

Paid features are charged in tokens, which you buy through our payment gateway. When you make a payment we receive and store the gateway's order and payment identifiers, the amount, and the resulting token credit — never your card, UPI or bank details, which are handled entirely by the gateway under its own privacy policy.

We maintain a ledger of every token credit and debit on your account. Where a verification cannot be completed, the tokens charged are refunded automatically and both entries appear in that ledger.

07 Why we use your data

We process your personal data on the following bases:

  • To provide the service you asked for — creating your account, signing you in, running verifications, and maintaining your wallet.
  • With your consent — Aadhaar-based identity verification, which you initiate and confirm by entering an OTP.
  • To meet legal obligations — retaining KYC and transaction records where Indian law requires it.
  • For our legitimate interests — preventing fraud and abuse, securing the service, and diagnosing faults.

09 Where data is stored, and for how long

Your data is stored on cloud infrastructure located in India. Some providers named above may process limited data outside India under their own contractual safeguards.

  • Account and KYC records — kept while your account is active, and afterwards only for as long as law requires us to retain them.
  • Verification history and wallet ledger — retained as financial and audit records.
  • One-time passwords — stored only as a hash, and deleted automatically minutes after issue.
  • Sessions — revoked when you sign out and expire automatically.
  • Server logs — retained for a limited period for security and debugging.

10 How we protect your data

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit.
  • One-time passwords and Aadhaar identifiers are stored only as irreversible hashes, never in readable form.
  • Access to production systems is restricted to authorised personnel.
  • Sessions use short-lived access tokens with revocable refresh tokens tied to your device.
  • Rate limiting and audit logging guard against abuse of verification and login endpoints.

No system can be guaranteed completely secure. If a breach occurs that is likely to affect you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as required by law.

11 Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and how it is processed.
  • Have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected or updated.
  • Request erasure of your data, subject to records we must retain by law.
  • Withdraw consent you previously gave, including for Aadhaar verification. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer offer parts of the service.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf if you are unable to.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied with our response.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in §15.
